Hi, I’m Ben. Online I’m The Benn.

I’m a creative web developer. I build websites, interactive 3D and tools for people who want their thing to feel made, not generated.

I didn’t start with code. I started with a camera, then music, then live television. Every one of those taught me something I still use when I build for a client.

How I got here

  1. 1

    The camera

    Short films with friends and photos of whatever the light was doing. Film taught me framing and timing, and that nothing is finished until someone else watches it. Photography taught me to wait for the frame.

  2. 2

    The beats

    The films needed sound, so I opened FL Studio and stayed for years. Drill beats, cold keys, heavy 808s. A loop is a hundred tiny decisions you only notice when one is wrong. My tracks are out as The Benn on Spotify and SoundCloud.

    Kick and 808, 1:2play the pads

  3. 3

    Live TV

    I help cover live soccer for SNL and NZS broadcasts, and I help with the software development behind them. In live production there is no second take. Things have to work the first time, every time, which is a good standard for websites too.

  4. 4

    Code

    Now I study computer science at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science (FRI) in Ljubljana. Java, databases, operating systems, networks, statistics, and ARM assembly on real hardware. Outside class I build games, tools and websites, like this one.

Working with me

  • You talk to the person building it. No account managers, no hand-offs.
  • You see it early. Work in progress lives on a private link, so you can click around instead of reading reports.
  • It stays yours. The code, the domain and the hosting accounts end up in your name.
  • AI where it helps. I use AI coding tools to move faster. The design decisions, the testing and the responsibility stay with me.
