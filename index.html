Live soccer on Slovenian TV

I help cover live SNL and NZS matches, and I help with the software development behind the broadcasts.

The lab

Small instruments that run in your browser. Every sound is synthesized live and nothing is downloaded.

  • XY scopeTwo sine waves, one in each ear, drawn as a picture. The trace at the top of every page on this site is this instrument.Open
  • Beat padsEight pads, four drums and a 16-step loop. No samples: every sound is built from oscillators and noise while you play.Open
  • Vision mixerThe basic controls of a live TV switcher: pick the next shot on preview, then cut, mix or wipe it to air.Open
  • ViewfinderWhat a broadcast camera operator sees: framing guides, safe areas, exposure warnings and running timecode, over one of my own stadium frames.Open
  • Blinking LEDs in ARM assemblyThe first program anyone writes for a microcontroller, in assembly. Step through it line by line and watch the board light up.Open
